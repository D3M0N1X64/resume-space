01 / About

      
About Me


      

        

          

            As network administrator at Alabama Goodwill Industries, I keep the network and the
            people who depend on it connected and running. I came up through hands-on support:
            five years as a remote Mac+ technician for Apple, three years as an enrollment counselor at
            Capella University (with stretch roles in operations and IT), and field and desktop work
            along the way.
          

          

            I enjoy problems that need both halves of the brain: tracking down why a network is
            misbehaving, designing a database that stays tidy, writing code that saves someone an
            afternoon, or planning a project so it lands on time. I'm now a senior in Capella
            University's ABET-accredited Information Technology program.
          

          

            Away from the rack, you'll find me on a motorcycle, playing guitar, or flying simulated
            F-16C and F/A-18C missions in DCS World, MSFS and War Thunder (with the odd Ace Combat
            sortie).
